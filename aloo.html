Aloo Paratha

A delicious Indian flatbread stuffed with a flavorful potato filling and cooked until golden and crispy.

⏱️
Preparation 20 mins
🔥
Cooking 20 mins
👨‍👩‍👧‍👦
Servings 4 People

🥕 Ingredients

  • Wheat Flour 2 cups
  • Potatoes 4
  • Onion 1
  • Green Chilli 2
  • Coriander Leaves 2 tbsp
  • Water As needed

👩‍🍳 Preparation

Prepare a soft dough using wheat flour. Boil and mash the potatoes, mix them with onion, green chilli and coriander leaves. Stuff the potato mixture inside the dough and roll it into a paratha before cooking.

🍳 How to Make Aloo Paratha

01

Prepare the Dough

Mix wheat flour with water and prepare a soft dough. Keep it aside for a few minutes.

02

Prepare the Filling

Boil the potatoes and mash them. Add chopped onion, green chilli and coriander leaves.

03

Stuff the Dough

Take a portion of dough, place the potato filling inside and seal it properly.

04

Roll the Paratha

Gently roll the stuffed dough into a round paratha.

05

Cook and Serve

Cook the paratha on a hot pan until both sides are golden and cooked. Serve hot.
