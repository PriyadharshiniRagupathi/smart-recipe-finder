Chole Bhature

A popular North Indian dish made with spicy chickpea curry served with soft and fluffy deep-fried bhature.

⏱️
Preparation 30 mins
🔥
Cooking 30 mins
👨‍👩‍👧‍👦
Servings 4 People

🥕 Ingredients

  • Chickpeas 2 cups
  • Maida 2 cups
  • Onion 2
  • Tomatoes 2
  • Potatoes 2
  • Curd 1/2 cup

👩‍🍳 Preparation

Soak the chickpeas and cook them until soft. Prepare a spicy gravy using onion, tomatoes and potatoes, then add the cooked chickpeas. Prepare a soft dough using maida and curd, roll it into bhature and deep fry until fluffy and golden.

🍳 How to Make Chole Bhature

01

Prepare the Chickpeas

Soak the chickpeas in water for several hours. Cook them until they become soft.

02

Prepare the Chole

Cook chopped onion and tomatoes until soft. Add potatoes and the required spices.

03

Add the Chickpeas

Add the cooked chickpeas to the prepared gravy. Mix well and cook until the flavours combine.

04

Prepare the Bhature

Mix maida with curd and water to prepare a soft dough. Roll small portions into round bhature.

05

Cook and Serve

Deep fry the rolled bhature until they become fluffy and golden. Serve hot with spicy chole.
