Bread Omelette

A quick and tasty breakfast made with eggs, bread, onion and green chilli, cooked together until golden.

⏱️
Preparation 5 mins
🔥
Cooking 10 mins
👨‍👩‍👧‍👦
Servings 2 People

🥕 Ingredients

  • Bread 4 slices
  • Eggs 4
  • Onion 1
  • Green Chilli 2
  • Coriander Leaves 2 tbsp
  • Milk 2 tbsp

👩‍🍳 Preparation

Beat the eggs with chopped onion, green chilli and coriander leaves. Dip the bread slices in the egg mixture and cook them on a hot pan until golden and the egg is fully cooked.

🍳 How to Make Bread Omelette

01

Prepare the Egg Mixture

Beat the eggs in a bowl and add chopped onion, green chilli and coriander leaves.

02

Add Milk

Add a small amount of milk to the egg mixture and mix everything well.

03

Dip the Bread

Dip each bread slice into the egg mixture and coat both sides evenly.

04

Cook the Bread

Place the coated bread on a hot pan and cook until the egg becomes firm and golden.

05

Serve Hot

Cook both sides evenly and serve the bread omelette while it is warm.
