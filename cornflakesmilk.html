Cornflakes with Milk

A quick and simple breakfast made with crispy cornflakes served with fresh milk and sweet fruits.

⏱️
Preparation 5 mins
🔥
Cooking 0 mins
👨‍👩‍👧‍👦
Servings 2 People

🥕 Ingredients

  • Cornflakes 2 cups
  • Milk 2 cups
  • Banana 1
  • Apple 1
  • Honey 2 tbsp
  • Nuts 2 tbsp

👩‍🍳 Preparation

Add crispy cornflakes to a bowl and pour chilled milk over them. Add sliced banana, apple and nuts. Drizzle with honey and serve immediately for a quick breakfast.

🍳 How to Make Cornflakes with Milk

01

Prepare the Fruits

Wash and slice the banana and apple into small pieces.

02

Add the Cornflakes

Place the required amount of crispy cornflakes in a serving bowl.

03

Add the Milk

Pour chilled milk over the cornflakes until they are lightly covered.

04

Add Fruits and Nuts

Add the sliced fruits and nuts over the cornflakes and milk.

05

Serve Immediately

Drizzle with honey and serve immediately while the cornflakes are still crispy.
