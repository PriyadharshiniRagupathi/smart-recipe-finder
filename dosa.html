Masala Dosa

A crispy and delicious South Indian dosa filled with flavorful potato masala.

⏱️
Preparation 20 mins
🔥
Cooking 20 mins
👨‍👩‍👧‍👦
Servings 4 People

🥕 Ingredients

  • Dosa Batter 4 cups
  • Potatoes 4
  • Onion 2
  • Green Chilli 2
  • Ginger 1 small piece

👩‍🍳 Preparation

Prepare the potato filling by cooking the potatoes and mixing them with onion, green chilli and ginger. Keep the filling ready before making the dosa.

🍳 How to Make Masala Dosa

01

Prepare the Potato Filling

Cook the potatoes, peel them and mash them lightly.

02

Prepare the Masala

Cook the onion, green chilli and ginger and mix them with the mashed potatoes.

03

Heat the Tawa

Heat a dosa tawa properly and spread a thin layer of dosa batter over it.

04

Make the Dosa

Cook the dosa until it becomes crispy and golden. Place the potato filling in the center.

05

Serve

Fold the dosa and serve hot with sambar and coconut chutney.
