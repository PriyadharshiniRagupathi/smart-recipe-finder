French Toast

A simple and delicious breakfast made with bread dipped in a sweet egg mixture and cooked until golden and soft.

⏱️
Preparation 5 mins
🔥
Cooking 10 mins
👨‍👩‍👧‍👦
Servings 2 People

🥕 Ingredients

  • Bread 4 slices
  • Eggs 2
  • Milk 1/2 cup
  • Sugar 2 tbsp
  • Vanilla Essence 1 tsp
  • Butter 2 tbsp

👩‍🍳 Preparation

Beat the eggs with milk, sugar and vanilla essence. Dip the bread slices into the mixture and cook them on a hot pan with butter until both sides become golden and lightly crisp.

🍳 How to Make French Toast

01

Prepare the Egg Mixture

Beat the eggs in a bowl and add milk, sugar and vanilla essence. Mix everything well.

02

Dip the Bread

Dip each bread slice into the egg mixture and coat both sides evenly.

03

Heat the Pan

Heat a pan and add a small amount of butter. Allow it to melt evenly.

04

Cook the Toast

Place the coated bread on the pan and cook both sides until golden and lightly crisp.

05

Serve Hot

Transfer the French toast to a plate and serve warm with your preferred topping.
