Pancakes

Soft and fluffy pancakes made with flour, milk and eggs, cooked until golden and served warm with sweet toppings.

⏱️
Preparation 10 mins
🔥
Cooking 15 mins
👨‍👩‍👧‍👦
Servings 4 People

🥕 Ingredients

  • All-Purpose Flour 2 cups
  • Milk 1 1/2 cups
  • Eggs 2
  • Sugar 2 tbsp
  • Butter 2 tbsp
  • Banana 1

👩‍🍳 Preparation

Mix flour, milk, eggs and sugar to prepare a smooth batter. Heat a pan and pour the batter into small rounds. Cook both sides until golden and fluffy. Serve warm with your preferred toppings.

🍳 How to Make Pancakes

01

Prepare the Batter

Mix flour, milk, eggs and sugar in a bowl to make a smooth and slightly thick batter.

02

Heat the Pan

Heat a pan over medium heat and lightly grease it with butter.

03

Pour the Batter

Pour a small amount of batter onto the hot pan and spread it gently into a round shape.

04

Cook Both Sides

Cook until bubbles appear on the surface, then flip and cook the other side until golden.

05

Serve Warm

Stack the pancakes on a plate and serve warm with banana or your preferred topping.
