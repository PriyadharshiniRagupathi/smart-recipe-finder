Ven Pongal

A comforting and delicious South Indian dish made with rice and moong dal, perfect for a wholesome breakfast.

⏱️
Preparation 10 mins
🔥
Cooking 20 mins
👨‍👩‍👧‍👦
Servings 4 People

🥕 Ingredients

  • Rice 1 cup
  • Moong Dal ½ cup
  • Ginger 1 small piece
  • Cashews 10
  • Ghee 2 tbsp

👩‍🍳 Preparation

Wash the rice and moong dal well. Cook them together until soft and creamy. Prepare the seasoning separately and mix it with the cooked rice and dal.

🍳 How to Make Ven Pongal

01

Wash the Rice and Dal

Wash the rice and moong dal thoroughly and keep them ready.

02

Cook

Cook the rice and moong dal together with enough water until they become soft.

03

Prepare the Seasoning

Heat ghee and prepare the seasoning with ginger and cashews.

04

Mix

Add the seasoning to the cooked rice and dal mixture and mix everything well.

05

Serve

Serve hot with coconut chutney or sambar.
