Poori Masala

Crispy and fluffy deep-fried pooris served with a delicious and flavorful potato masala.

⏱️
Preparation 15 mins
🔥
Cooking 20 mins
👨‍👩‍👧‍👦
Servings 4 People

🥕 Ingredients

  • Wheat Flour 2 cups
  • Potatoes 4
  • Onion 2
  • Green Chilli 2
  • Ginger 1 small piece

👩‍🍳 Preparation

Prepare a firm dough using wheat flour and water. Make the potato masala separately and keep it ready. Roll the dough into small circles before frying.

🍳 How to Make Poori Masala

01

Prepare the Dough

Mix wheat flour with water and prepare a smooth, firm dough.

02

Prepare Potato Masala

Cook the potatoes and mix them with onion, green chilli and ginger.

03

Roll the Pooris

Divide the dough into small portions and roll each portion into a small circle.

04

Fry

Carefully fry the rolled dough until the pooris puff up and become golden.

05

Serve

Serve hot and crispy pooris with the prepared potato masala.
