Idli

Soft, fluffy and delicious South Indian steamed rice cakes.

⏱️
Preparation 15 mins
🔥
Cooking 15 mins
👨‍👩‍👧‍👦
Servings 4 People

🥕 Ingredients

  • Idli Rice 2 cups
  • Urad Dal 1 cup
  • Poha ½ cup
  • Water As needed

👩‍🍳 Preparation

Soak the rice and urad dal separately. Grind them into a smooth batter, mix them together and allow the batter to ferment before steaming.

🍳 How to Make Idli

01

Soak

Wash and soak the rice and urad dal separately for several hours.

02

Grind

Grind the soaked rice and urad dal into a smooth batter.

03

Ferment

Mix the batter well and allow it to ferment until it becomes light and fluffy.

04

Steam

Pour the batter into idli moulds and steam until the idlis are cooked.

05

Serve

Remove the idlis carefully and serve hot.
