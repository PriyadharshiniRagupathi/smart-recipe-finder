Vegetable Sandwich

A fresh and tasty sandwich filled with colorful vegetables and served between crispy and golden bread slices.

⏱️
Preparation 10 mins
🔥
Cooking 10 mins
👨‍👩‍👧‍👦
Servings 2 People

🥕 Ingredients

  • Bread 4 slices
  • Potatoes 2
  • Carrot 1
  • Cucumber 1
  • Tomato 1
  • Onion 1

👩‍🍳 Preparation

Boil and mash the potatoes. Slice the carrot, cucumber, tomato and onion. Spread the potato mixture over the bread and arrange the vegetables evenly. Toast the sandwich until the bread becomes crisp and golden.

🍳 How to Make Vegetable Sandwich

01

Prepare the Potato Filling

Boil the potatoes, mash them well and keep the mixture ready for the sandwich.

02

Prepare the Vegetables

Slice the carrot, cucumber, tomato and onion into thin pieces.

03

Assemble the Sandwich

Place the potato filling and sliced vegetables evenly over a bread slice.

04

Toast the Sandwich

Place the sandwich on a hot pan or sandwich toaster and cook until both sides are golden.

05

Serve Hot

Cut the sandwich into pieces and serve warm with your preferred accompaniment.
